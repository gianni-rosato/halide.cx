Iris-AVIF

Iris-AVIF is our AVIF encoder, built on the perceptual research behind Iris-WebP. At equal quality it compresses 21% better than libaom, and it encodes faster. It also handles film grain, HDR, and screen content. The output is standard AVIF.

An encoder that adapts.

Iris-AVIF takes the methods behind Iris-WebP and applies them to AV1's much larger set of intra-coding tools. It detects what kind of image it has been given and changes how it encodes it. The files it writes are standard AVIF, which decodes in more than 95% of web browsers.

Grain synthesis
Iris-AVIF detects grain it can reproduce synthetically and decides how much to add, from coarse film grain to fine digital chroma noise, with no manual settings.
HDR
AVIF stores HDR natively, without the layering tricks that JPEG and WebP need. On our HDR test image, Iris-AVIF keeps more detail than SVT-AV1 and encodes 5 times faster.
Screen content
Compresses screenshots and other screen content up to 415% better than JPEG.
Perceptual tune
The default tune targets what people see, including improvements that metrics fail to reward.

Iris-AVIF or Iris-WebP.

Iris-AVIF compresses 20% better than Iris-WebP at the same quality and speed, and it adds grain synthesis and HDR. AVIF decodes in more than 95% of web browsers, 1.47% fewer than WebP.

Iris-WebP is mature and still improving alongside Iris-AVIF. If you already ship WebP or need the widest decoder support, it drops into your pipeline unchanged.
