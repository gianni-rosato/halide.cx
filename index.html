Iris-AVIF

Our most efficient encoder. Iris-AVIF brings the research behind Iris-WebP to AV1's larger toolset, and adds grain synthesis and HDR.

Iris-WebP

A WebP encoder tuned for the human visual system. Compared to libwebp, the reference encoder, Iris-WebP produces smaller files at the same visual quality and the same encode time.

See the full results
27% Smaller files than libwebp at equal CVVDP score and equal speed. 13% by SSIMULACRA2, 29% by MS-SSIM.
26% Smaller files than libjpeg-turbo at equal CVVDP score. 24% by SSIMULACRA2, 35% by MS-SSIM.
Drop-in Output is standard WebP. Every browser that shows WebP today shows Iris output unchanged.
