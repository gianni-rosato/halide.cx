Why Iris

A Better WebP Encoder

Images encoded with Iris-WebP look significantly better than those encoded with the reference WebP encoder, and Iris-WebP's performance outclasses encoders for slower, newer Web-first formats like AVIF. WebP support is also currently ubiquitous, and large websites serve billions of WebP images every single day.
