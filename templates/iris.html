Iris

Iris-WebP is a WebP encoder tuned for how people see images. At the same encode time as libwebp's default setting, it produces files up to 27% smaller at equal quality. The output is standard WebP and decodes everywhere WebP already does.

why Iris

A Better WebP Encoder

WebP is supported by every major browser, and large sites serve billions of WebP images every day. Iris-WebP keeps the format and replaces the encoder. Switching means smaller files at the quality you already ship, or higher quality at the file sizes you already pay for, with no change to decoders, CDNs, or markup.
