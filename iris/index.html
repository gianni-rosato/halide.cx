Why Iris

A Better WebP Encoder

Images encoded with Iris-WebP look significantly better than those encoded with the reference WebP encoder, and Iris-WebP's performance outclasses encoders for slower, newer Web-first formats like AVIF. WebP support is also currently ubiquitous, and large websites serve billions of WebP images every single day.

Fast

Iris-WebP is faster than competing modern Web codecs while still delivering exceptional quality.

Efficient

Achieve up to 50% better compression than JPEG, leading to faster page load times & reduced bandwidth.

High Quality

Iris-WebP is designed with the human visual system in mind, allowing up to 17% better compression than the reference WebP encoder.
